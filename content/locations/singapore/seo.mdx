**How long does SEO take to work?**
Google Maps rankings often move within 4–8 weeks. Organic rankings typically take 3–6 months. Competitive keywords can take 6–12 months.

**What's the difference between an SEO agency and an SEO consultant?**
An agency or company does the work for you. A consultant advises and audits but doesn't usually execute. Epicware is an agency — our team does the work, on our own software.

**Can an SEO agency guarantee first-page rankings?**
No. Rankings depend on competition and Google's own algorithm. What a good agency can promise is a transparent process, dated proof of past results, and live reporting.

**What's the difference between SEO and GEO?**
SEO wins rankings in Google's organic results. GEO (generative engine optimisation) wins citations in AI answers like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews. See [AI search visibility](/locations/singapore/ai-search-visibility).

**Should I start with SEO or Google Ads?**
Google Ads brings leads immediately but stops the moment you stop paying. SEO compounds over time. Many Singapore businesses run both together, especially early on.

**Do you work with multi-outlet businesses?**
Yes. Every outlet gets its own keyword strategy, rank tracking and reporting, managed from one dashboard.

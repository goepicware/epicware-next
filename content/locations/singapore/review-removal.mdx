## Pain

Singapore businesses face a unique problem: their entire local customer base is concentrated in a few square kilometres. One bad review is visible to every potential customer in your district. A competitor with a grudge, a non-customer who got the wrong business, or a review that violates Google's content policies can sit on your profile for months unless you know exactly how to challenge it. Most SMB owners try the flag button, get a generic rejection, and give up.

## Promise

Epicware doesn't give up after one rejection. We submit the policy violation with the correct legal and procedural framing, escalate through the right channels, and keep following up until the review is removed or Google gives a final ruling.

## What's Included

Reviews that qualify for removal:

- **Fake reviews** posted by competitors, strangers, or bots with no real customer relationship
- **Malicious reviews** from ex-employees, disputed business relationships, or targeted harassment campaigns
- **Policy-violating reviews** containing personal attacks, offensive language, spam, or false factual claims
- **Misattributed reviews** left on the wrong business profile by a customer who confused two locations

If a review is simply negative but genuine — a real customer who had a bad experience and is entitled to their opinion — it will not qualify for removal. Our first step is always a free assessment to identify which reviews are actually removable before any work begins.

**The process:**

1. **Assessment** — we review the flagged review, identify the specific Google policy violation, and confirm eligibility
2. **Submission** — a formal removal request through Google's business channels with the correct violation framing
3. **Escalation** — if the first request is rejected, we escalate through additional channels and re-submit with supplementary evidence
4. **Follow-through** — we track the case until Google issues a final resolution

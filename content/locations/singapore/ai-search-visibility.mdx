## Pain

A growing share of local queries are now answered directly by AI systems — Google's AI Overviews, ChatGPT with web search, and Perplexity — before a user ever clicks a standard search result. The businesses that appear in these AI-generated answers are there because their content and profiles are structured in ways AI systems can read, trust, and cite. Businesses that aren't optimised for this are invisible in the answer, regardless of their traditional SEO rank. ChatGPT can also [make up citations](/blog/does-chatgpt-make-up-citations) — which cuts both ways if you're not actively monitoring what it says about your business.

## Promise

GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) is the discipline of optimising for this new layer of search. It sits alongside traditional local SEO — not replacing it, but adding the AI visibility layer that increasingly determines which businesses customers discover first.

## FAQ

**What is GEO?**
Generative Engine Optimisation — structuring your content and Google Business Profile so AI systems like Google's AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity can read, trust, and cite your business when answering a customer's question.

**What is AEO?**
Answer Engine Optimisation — structured data and content formatting that helps AI assistants confidently recommend your business by name, rather than giving a vague or generic answer.

**How is this different from regular SEO?**
Regular SEO targets Google's organic ranked results. GEO and AEO target the AI-generated answer itself, which increasingly appears above or instead of those results.

**Can you guarantee my business will be cited by ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews?**
No one can guarantee a specific AI citation — the underlying models change. What we do is structure your signals correctly and monitor what AI tools are actually saying about you, so issues get caught and corrected.

## Pain

Singapore is dense, and local results change with where the searcher stands. Two searchers 2 km apart, in Tampines and Pasir Ris, can see a different top three for the same search. Customers search by MRT station, mall and estate — "dentist near Toa Payoh MRT," not "dentist Singapore."

Businesses slip in local search for five recurring reasons: a name, address or phone number that differs across directories; being missing from the directories Singapore customers actually use; too few reviews, stale reviews, or unanswered reviews; no page on the website for each outlet; and competitors accumulating more local mentions and links.

## Promise

Local SEO services help a business rank when people nearby search on Google Search and Google Maps. Google ranks local results mainly on three things — relevance, distance and prominence. You can't move your shop closer to every customer. Local SEO improves the other two: how well your business matches the search, and how well-known it is.

**Do Google reviews help local SEO?**
Yes. Google says more reviews and positive ratings can help your local ranking, and advises replying to them. Google's policy bans paid or rewarded reviews and asking only happy customers; we do neither.

**Do I need a physical shop for local SEO?**
No, but you must meet customers in person. Service-area businesses, like aircon servicing and movers, should hide their address on Google and list the areas they serve instead — we track those areas.

**Can you manage local SEO for multiple outlets?**
Yes. Each outlet gets its own keyword list, review tracking and rank grid, side by side in one dashboard.

**How do I choose a local SEO agency or tool in Singapore?**

## Pain

Ranking #1 isn't the finish line anymore. In the US, Google now answers many questions itself, before a single click.

In a Pew Research Center study of 900 US adults' Google searches, users clicked a traditional result link on 8% of visits when an AI summary appeared, compared with 15% when it didn't. The same Pew study found that about 18% of the Google searches analyzed produced an AI summary, and that longer, question-style searches were more likely to trigger one.

Google announced at I/O in May 2025 that AI Mode, its conversational search experience, was rolling out to all Google Search users in the US.

Old-school SEO, meaning thin keyword pages and links from anywhere, doesn't survive that. Neither do sites that mass-produce near-identical pages. Google's spam policies prohibit scaled content abuse: mass-producing pages mainly to manipulate rankings rather than to help users.

## FAQ

### Is SEO still worth it if Google shows AI answers?

Yes, but the target has changed. AI Overviews and AI Mode build their answers from websites Google trusts. Good SEO now earns you the click *and* the citation, while weak SEO loses both.

### How is US SEO different from local SEO?

Local SEO wins the Map Pack and "near me" searches around your locations. Website SEO wins organic results across the US cities and topics you target, through your pages, content and authority. Most US businesses with a physical presence need both.

### Can't we just create a page for every city?

Only where you genuinely serve that city and have something specific to say. Mass-producing near-identical city pages is a pattern Google treats as spam. We build location pages one at a time, each with real local substance.

### How long does SEO take in the US?

Technical fixes can show within weeks. Competitive US keywords usually take several months of consistent content and authority building to move.

### Do you guarantee #1 rankings?

No honest agency can guarantee Google's results. We commit to the work, and to reporting the rankings, traffic and leads it produces.

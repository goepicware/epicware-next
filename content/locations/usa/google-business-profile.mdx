## Pain

US business owners get told to stuff keywords into their business name ("Bob's Plumbing Dallas 24/7 Emergency"), list a home address, or set a service area covering the whole state. All three break Google's guidelines.

Profiles get suspended for this every day. When a profile goes, the calls stop overnight, and getting it back can take weeks of appeals and verification videos.

Local SEO practitioners report that service-area businesses with a hidden address don't need signage during video verification, and that overlapping service areas across listings can trigger suspension — exactly the kind of detail that gets missed by a DIY setup.

Meanwhile, the profiles that do play by the rules are often half-finished:

- wrong primary category,
- no services listed,
- photos from 2019,
- no posts.

## FAQ

### Can I put keywords in my business name to rank higher?

Not unless they're part of your real-world name. Google requires your profile name to match the name on your signage, website and stationery. Keyword-stuffed names are a common suspension trigger, and profiles get reported by competitors for it.

### I work from home. Should I show my address?

No. If customers don't visit you, Google says to hide your address and set a service area instead. You can still rank in the Map Pack for the areas you serve.

### How big can my service area be?

Google says it shouldn't extend more than about two hours' drive from where your business is based. Draw it around where you actually work, and avoid overlapping areas between multiple listings.

### My profile was suspended. Can you help?

Yes. We identify the likely cause, fix the profile so it complies, and prepare the appeal and any verification video Google requests. Reinstatement is Google's decision, so we can't guarantee the outcome.

### What happened to "Google Guaranteed"?

Google discontinued the Google Guarantee badge and its money-back guarantee in November 2025, moving Local Services Ads to a "Google Verified" badge. It's part of Google's paid Local Services Ads, not your free Business Profile.

### How is this different from your Local SEO service?

This service focuses on your Google Business Profile itself. Local SEO covers your whole local presence, including Apple Maps, Bing, citations, location pages and metro-wide rank tracking. Many US businesses start here, then add Local SEO.

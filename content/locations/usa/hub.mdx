Epicware's team is based in Singapore and serves US businesses online. Platform reports, evidence and follow-up don't depend on where we sit; your profile does.

In BrightLocal's 2026 US survey, 97% of consumers read reviews for local businesses, and 31% will only use a business rated 4.5 stars or higher, up from 17% a year earlier — which is exactly why we're starting with review removal: it's the fastest way to stop a fake or policy-violating review from costing you that next customer.

The FTC's rule on consumer reviews and testimonials (16 CFR Part 465) took effect on 21 October 2024 and lets the FTC seek civil penalties for fake reviews. The same rule bans buying positive or negative reviews and using groundless legal threats or intimidation to suppress negative reviews. The Consumer Review Fairness Act voids non-disparagement clauses in consumer form contracts, so a business cannot contract its way out of honest negative reviews. Every report we file for a US client follows those rules — we only ever act on reviews that break a platform's own content policy, never simply because a rating is low.

US clients are billed in USD.

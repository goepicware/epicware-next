## Pain

A one-star review sits unanswered for a week. A prospect reads it, sees the silence, and calls your competitor. That's how most US businesses lose customers to reviews: not the review, but the silence.

In BrightLocal's 2026 survey, 89% of consumers expected business owners to respond to reviews, and 42% said they were unlikely to use a business that ignores them. Yet most owners check reviews when they remember to, reply with copy-paste lines, or don't reply at all.

The US adds two traps that catch businesses every year:

- **Healthcare replies that break HIPAA.** The HHS Office for Civil Rights fined a Dallas dental practice USD 10,000 for disclosing a patient's protected health information in replies to Yelp reviews. A North Carolina dental practice was fined USD 50,000 by HHS OCR after its reply to a negative Google review included the patient's name, condition and treatment details.
- **Yelp's rules.** Yelp's guidelines tell business owners not to ask customers for reviews, unlike Google, which allows requests as long as all customers are treated equally. A review campaign that's fine on Google can backfire on Yelp.

### HIPAA and reviews: what US healthcare practices need to know

When a patient posts a review, the practice still can't confirm they're a patient, or discuss their condition, treatment or bill in public. A safe reply thanks the reviewer and invites them to contact the office directly, and that's all. Our healthcare reply rules are built around that limit.

## FAQ

### Should we reply to every review, even the positive ones?

Yes. Consumers expect replies, and seeing a business engage with all its reviews, not just complaints, builds trust. Keep positive replies short and specific, never copy-paste.

### We're a dental or medical practice. Can we reply to reviews at all?

Yes, carefully. Don't confirm the reviewer is a patient or mention any treatment, visit, insurance or billing detail, even if they shared it themselves. A thank-you plus an invitation to contact the office is the safe pattern.

### Can we ask customers for Yelp reviews?

Yelp's guidelines tell businesses not to. Google allows review requests as long as you don't single out happy customers.

### Can AI reply to reviews for us?

AI drafts the reply; you stay in control. Drafts are written to sound personal and reflect what the reviewer actually said. For sensitive or negative reviews, a human approves before anything is posted.

### What about fake reviews?

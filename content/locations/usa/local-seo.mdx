## FAQ

### Is Google still the only local search that matters in the US?

It's the biggest, but not the only one. US consumers now check several platforms, and AI assistants increasingly answer "best [service] near me" questions. A complete local presence covers Google, Apple Maps, Bing and the major review sites.

### Do I need Apple Maps if my customers use Google?

Many of your customers use both. Apple Maps powers Siri and the Maps app on every iPhone. Claiming your place card through Apple Business Connect is free and takes the guesswork out of what iPhone users see.

### How does Bing affect ChatGPT?

ChatGPT's web results draw on Bing, and early research suggests Bing Places listings influence what it finds. An accurate Bing listing, imported from your Google profile, is cheap insurance for AI visibility.

### We serve customers at their homes. Can we still rank?

Yes. Service-area businesses rank in the Map Pack without showing an address. The setup has to follow Google's service-area rules, and your pages and citations need to reflect the areas you serve.

### How do you measure results across a whole metro?

EpicMap places a grid of points across your service area and checks your ranking from each one, weekly. You see a heat map of where you rank, not one number from one location.

### How long does local SEO take in the US?

Profile and citation fixes often show in local rankings within 30–60 days. Competitive metros take longer, and organic website rankings usually take several months.

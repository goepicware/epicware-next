## Pain

"What's the best dentist near me?" More Americans now ask that question to ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI Mode, and the AI names two or three businesses. It never shows them a page two.

In BrightLocal's 2026 US survey, AI search engines ranked as the third most-used source of local business recommendations, behind Facebook and Google. ChatGPT reached about 900 million weekly active users in February 2026.

AI answers don't hand out ten blue links; they recommend a shortlist. Pew Research found that when Google showed an AI summary, users clicked a link inside the summary on just 1% of visits, so being named in the answer matters more than the click. If the AI doesn't mention you, most people never learn you exist.

### Why AI visibility is different from SEO

SEO earns a position in a list. AI visibility earns a *recommendation*. AI tools weigh consistency, reviews and third-party mentions more heavily than any single page ranking, which is why a business can rank on Google and still be invisible to ChatGPT.

Google's AI Mode shows place cards for local businesses such as restaurants, salons and stores, with ratings, reviews and opening hours — one more surface where consistent facts decide whether you're the one shown. Bing Places listings influence the web results ChatGPT draws on, according to early research from Whitespark, which is why an accurate Bing listing matters even for a ChatGPT-focused strategy.

## FAQ

### Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend us?

No. AI tools decide their own answers, and they change. What we control is the evidence those tools read: consistent facts, credible mentions, strong reviews and quotable content. We track the results monthly so you can see the trend.

### Which AI tools matter in the US?

ChatGPT, Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot and Perplexity. We track the ones your customers are most likely to use for your category.

### Does my Google ranking affect AI answers?

Partly. Google's AI features draw on sites Google trusts, while ChatGPT draws on Bing's web results and other sources. Strong SEO helps, but AI visibility also depends on consistency and mentions across the web.

### How long until AI tools mention us?

It varies by tool and category. Some changes show within weeks of facts being corrected; authority-driven recommendations take longer. Monthly tracking shows you the direction.

### What's llms.txt?

A plain-text file on your website that summarizes your business for AI tools, much as a sitemap does for search engines. It's one of several signals we put in place.
